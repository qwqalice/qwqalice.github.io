abstract: >-
  Medical image segmentation relies on high-quality annotated data that is often difficult and costly to obtain. 
  To address this, we propose an Attention-Guided weak-to-Strong Consistency Match (AIGCMatch) framework 
  that incorporates attention-guided perturbation strategies at both the image level and feature level 
  to achieve weak-to-strong consistency regularization. This approach preserves the structural information 
  of medical images while enhancing the model's ability to process complex semantic information. 
  Extensive experiments on the ACDC and ISIC-2017 datasets validate the effectiveness of our method, 
  achieving a 90.4% Dice score on ACDC in the 7-case scenario and significantly outperforming baselines on ISIC-2017.
